About the academy

Coaching that treats you like an athlete.

Led by Shelly Mars — running junior and adult programs since 1990 — our certified coaches bring years of USTA and Division III collegiate experience to every lesson. We develop players athletically, mentally, and emotionally, with a progressive teaching method geared to each individual's ability.

You'll find us on twelve beautiful courts at The Roxbury Latin School's 117-acre campus in West Roxbury — a quiet corner surrounded by trees. We call it Tennis in the Woods.
